      Daily skincare guide

      Simple habits that help keep acne under control.

      Wash twice a day

      Use a gentle face wash morning and night. Do not scrub hard.

      Do not pop pimples

      Popping spreads bacteria and can leave scars.

      Use sunscreen

      Pick an oil-free sunscreen. Sun makes dark marks last longer.

      Keep hands off your face

      Clean your phone and pillowcase often.

      Drink water, eat fresh

      Less fried and sugary food, more fruit and vegetables.

      Know when to get help

      If acne is painful, spreading or leaves scars, see a dermatologist.

      How it works

      From photo to report in a few seconds.

      1. Upload

        Your photo goes to the Flask server for analysis.

      2. Detect

        The model finds acne spots and measures how much skin they cover.

      3. Rate

        Spot count and affected area decide the severity level.

      4. Act

        You get tips, medicine ideas and, for severe cases, a doctor list.
